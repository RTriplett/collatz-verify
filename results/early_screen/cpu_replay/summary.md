# Early CPU verification replay

Repeated CPU-only timings of captured post-sieve states. Repetitions are not independent root samples; no confidence interval or full-range GPU-year estimate is inferred.

Single-worker CPU and wall times include state decoding, validation, allocation, all bucket construction and exact verification. Resident tables are built separately. Fixture loading, GPU filtering/transfers, oracle comparisons and wide rechecks are excluded. Best configurations are descriptive selections from this fixture, not established population winners.
